# Phuket Smart Bus — Operations Design Direction

Design read: a field-intelligence instrument for Phuket transport—Braun restraint, air-traffic evidence discipline, and the island’s route geometry doing the visual work.

- Reference: Braun control surfaces and civil aviation operations boards, not a generic SaaS dashboard.
- Variance: 6/10. The map dominates; evidence and exceptions form the asymmetric rail.
- Motion: 3/10. Vehicles move because the underlying state changes; controls confirm action; data does not perform.
- Density: 7/10 on the operations wall, 5/10 on laptops, 4/10 on phones.
- Type: Source Sans 3 for language, Josefin Sans for the few display moments, JetBrains Mono only for aligned data.
- Colour: neutral structure; green is observed/available, amber is attention, red is loss or unavailable. Colour never substitutes for a label.
- Evidence rule: observed, modelled, inferred, and unavailable must be distinguishable without opening documentation.
- Hierarchy rule: current posture first, spatial evidence second, exceptions third, raw fields last.
- Blind-spot rule: every operational surface states what the system cannot observe.
