# Rider landing comprehension brief

- **Who:** a visitor leaving HKT arrivals, carrying luggage, deciding whether to wait for the bus and what ticket to buy.
- **One job:** choose a stop, understand the next real bus, then choose a single ride or three-day pass without learning the operator console.
- **Hierarchy:** next bus and stop first; recommendation and ticket action second; live map third; flight, model and research detail after the decision.
- **Constraints:** phone first, laptop and large displays, live-versus-modelled honesty, 44px primary targets, and no removal of the live map or earned evidence.
- **Direction:** the airport departure board beside a route map—warm ticket paper for the decision, dark cartography for supporting evidence, PKSB red only for action and urgency.

## Geometry budget

At desktop widths the passenger decision occupies 36% of the working region and the map keeps at least 60%. At 3384×1932 the old 440px rail consumed 13% while the map consumed 87%; the repaired decision region must be at least 900px and no more than 40% of the viewport. At 1440×900 and 1024×768 the recommendation and the first ticket action must begin inside the first screen. The map remains present and independently sized; supporting comparison detail is closed by default but fully readable and keyboard reachable.
